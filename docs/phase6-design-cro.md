# Phase 6 — Design & Conversion Enhancement

## Objective
Raise Tayseer’s website from a technically strong, accessible and SEO-ready platform to a more persuasive enterprise experience that communicates value quickly, builds confidence, and creates clearer paths to enquiry.

## Design guardrails
- Preserve the Tayseer logo, brand palette and established visual identity.
- Do not introduce generic AI-template layouts or unrelated visual trends.
- Keep the Phase 1–5 accessibility, SEO and performance architecture intact.
- Do not invent clients, certifications, metrics, partnerships or case-study outcomes.
- Any trust signal or proof point must come from existing verified website content.
- Motion must remain purposeful and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Phase 6 workstreams

### 1. Homepage value proposition
- Clarify who Tayseer serves, what it delivers, and why that matters within the first viewport.
- Improve primary/secondary CTA hierarchy.
- Add concise enterprise context around AI, digital banking and banking technology.

### 2. Enterprise credibility
- Improve presentation of existing proof, testimonials, regional presence and delivery capabilities.
- Make deployment models, expertise and operating approach easier to evaluate.
- Strengthen trust without adding unverifiable claims.

### 3. Solution discovery
- Improve solution-card hierarchy and decision support.
- Clarify relationships between core banking, AI, mobile banking, managed services and banking systems.
- Add contextual cross-links and conversion paths where appropriate.

### 4. Conversion architecture
- Use one clear primary enquiry action and a consistent secondary exploration action.
- Improve CTA copy, placement and visual hierarchy.
- Reduce dead ends across homepage, solution pages, blog and company pages.

### 5. Content hierarchy
- Improve headlines, section framing and supporting copy for executive scanning.
- Reduce generic marketing language where stronger product/business language already exists.
- Preserve SEO intent established in Phase 4.

### 6. Premium interaction polish
- Refine hover, active and focus feedback without increasing JavaScript cost.
- Preserve WCAG focus behavior from Phase 5.
- Avoid decorative motion that competes with content.

### 7. Responsive CRO
- Ensure CTA hierarchy, proof points and solution discovery remain strong on mobile/tablet.
- Avoid desktop-only persuasion patterns.

## Validation criteria
- No visual identity regression.
- No accessibility regression from the Phase 5 baseline.
- No SEO metadata or structured-data regression.
- Production build and typecheck pass.
- Homepage and key solution journeys are understandable without relying on animation.
- Primary conversion path is obvious on desktop and mobile.
- Final Lighthouse performance tuning remains deferred to the final optimization pass.
