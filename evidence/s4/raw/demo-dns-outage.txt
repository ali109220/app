--------------

has not lapsed and the delegation is intact. It simply has no address record and no MX record.

  1. Web: https://tayseerdemo.xyz/ is unreachable. curl exits 6 (CURLE_COULDNT_RESOLVE_HOST).

     as the contact/fallback in Footer.jsx, ContactSection.jsx, HomeContactForm.jsx, Connect.jsx,
     Careers.jsx, Legal.jsx and layout.tsx.
